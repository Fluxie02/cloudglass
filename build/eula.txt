3. PRIVACY

Cloudglass collects no data. It has no telemetry, analytics, crash
reporting or update checks, and the project runs no server.

Cloudglass stores only your app settings and SoundCloud's own cookies, cache
and site storage, locally on your PC in %APPDATA%\Cloudglass. Cookies are
encrypted at rest. You can erase this data at any time from the app, or when
uninstalling.

By default Cloudglass blocks third-party trackers on SoundCloud's website
and sends the Global Privacy Control signal. This can also hide some
third-party display ads. Tracker blocking can be switched off in the app.

The full privacy notice is in PRIVACY.md in the source repository.
